What the logos share

  • A large serif "iTECH" with a lowercase serif i in the campus color.
  • Fine horizontal lines cut across the base of the letters in TECH.
  • The campus color on the i and the middle line, a neutral on everything else.

Suggested usage

  • Use the on-dark versions on dark or photographic grounds and the inverse versions on white or light grounds.
  • Do not recolor, stretch or rotate the logos.
  • On a light ground, keep the Glades logo large: its blue line is low contrast on white.

Usage rules are suggestions, not official college guidelines.

Charcoal is my choice. The school's logos only use white lettering, so I picked #1D2024 for the inverse and single-color versions. If the college has an official dark color, such as a navy, tell me and I will swap it in.

Typography

The logos use a high-contrast old-style serif in the Garamond family: capitals for TECH, COLLEGE, GLADES and TECHNICAL COLLEGE, and a bold weight for "Immokalee Technical". This is a visual match; the original font is unconfirmed.

Aa
Headlines: EB Garamond

Fallbacks: Adobe Garamond Pro, Garamond, Georgia. Loaded from Google Fonts when online.

Aa
Text: Source Sans 3

A clean sans for reading text beside the serif headlines. Weights 400, 600 and 700.

The SVGs were traced from PNGs: the Glades logo from a 2021 px wide file (sharp) and the iTECH logo from a 416 px wide file (slightly softer, especially on fine hairlines). Lettering is outlined, so no font is required. For print or signage, request the original vector files from the college. The transparent PNGs of white-lettered logos will look blank on a white page.
